=============
Documentation
=============

Step-by-step guidance for submitting, finding and working with ENA data lives in the ENA documentation. The guides are grouped as follows:

`Submitting data <https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submit/general-guide.html>`_ — registering studies and samples, preparing files, and submitting reads, assemblies, targeted sequences and other analyses.

`Finding and downloading data <https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/retrieval/general-guide.html>`_ — exploring projects, downloading files, advanced search and programmatic access.

`Updating data <https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/update/metadata.html>`_ — editing metadata, assemblies and annotated sequences after submission.

`Tips and FAQs <https://ena-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/>`_ — release dates, metadata standards, taxonomy, common submission errors, data brokering, ORCID claiming and more.

EMBL-EBI also offers `training courses and materials on ENA <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/services/ena>`_, from introductory overviews to hands-on guidance on submission and data access.
