=================
Sample Checklists
=================

Introduction
============

ENA sample checklists define the minimum metadata required for sample registration, and every sample must conform to one
of them. These checklists have been developed with different research communities to reflect their metadata
requirements, and are hosted and versioned in `BioSamples <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biosamples/docs/guides/validation>`_.

Standards
=========

Our recommendation is that you use the checklist closest to your use case, rather than the default checklist,
since it will contain the metadata fields that your community considers important and will make your data more useful
to others.

List of checklists
==================

Explore all checklists `here <https://wwwdev.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/v2/checklists>`_.
