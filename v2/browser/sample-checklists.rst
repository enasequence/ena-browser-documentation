=================
Sample Checklists
=================

Introduction
============

ENA sample checklists define the minimum metadata required for sample registration, and every sample must conform to one
of them. These checklists have been developed with different research communities to reflect their varying metadata
requirements, and are hosted and versioned in BioSamples (`<https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biosamples/docs/guides/validation>`_).

Standards
=========

The strong preference is that you use a checklist closest to your use case, rather than the default checklist.
This is because it will contain the metadata fields considered to be important by your community peers and will make
your valuable data even more useful.
