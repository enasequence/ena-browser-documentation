======================
Governance and funding
======================

Governance
==========
ENA is developed and maintained at `EMBL-EBI <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/>`_, with guidance from the INSDC International Stakeholder Committee (`ISC <https://www.insdc.org/about-insdc/#vf-tabs__section-stakeholders-committee>`_) and the ENA Scientific Advisory Board (`SAB <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/leadership/advisory-boards/>`_).

Funding
=======
This project has received funding from the European Union's Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe research and innovation programme under grant agreement numbers AtlantECO [862923], AQUARIUS [101130915], BGE [101059492], BioOcean5D [101059915], Blue-Cloud 2026 [101094227], Contagio [101137283], eDNAqua-Plan [101112800], EVORA [101131959], MICROBE [10194353] and VEO [874735].

Further support has come from the Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council (BBSRC) Metagenomics Portal IV [BB/V01868X/1] and, in part, the Wellcome Trust [Darwin Tree of Life 226458/Z/22/Z]; the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation through Aquatic Symbiosis [MOORE-8897]; the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases of the National Institutes of Health [U24AI183840, Pathogen Data Network]; and the Novo Nordisk Foundation and Wellcome via the AEGIS grant [NNF24SA0092560]. This project has also received funding from the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL).
