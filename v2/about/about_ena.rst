About ENA
=========

What ENA is
===========

The European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) is an open repository for nucleotide sequence data. It brings together raw sequencing reads, sequence assemblies, functional annotation and associated metadata from studies across the life sciences, and provides the tools and services for submitting, managing and accessing that data. This makes ENA both a database of record and a platform for working with sequence data.

ENA is part of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC), a long-standing partnership between ENA, NCBI and DDBJ. Together, the three archives maintain a comprehensive, shared collection of nucleotide sequences and their annotation that safeguards the scientific record and supports the open exchange of sequence data worldwide.
