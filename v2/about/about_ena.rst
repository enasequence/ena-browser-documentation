About ENA
=========

What ENA is
===========

The European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) is a repository for all open nucleotide sequence data.
It brings together raw sequencing reads, sequence assemblies, functional annotation and associated metadata from
studies across the life sciences. ENA is part of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration
(`INSDC <https://www.insdc.org/>`_), a long-standing partnership between ENA, NCBI and DDBJ that captures and preserves
nucleotide sequences and their annotation. Together, the three archives maintain a comprehensive, shared collection that
safeguards the scientific record and supports the open exchange of sequence data worldwide.
